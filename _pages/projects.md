## Active Research Projects

### Polarimetric SAR for Flood Mapping (PhD Research)
**Institution:** University of Stirling | **Funding:** NERC  
**Duration:** 2022 – 2026

Developing novel methodologies for flood extent mapping using polarimetric 
Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) data. This research exploits polarimetric 
information from satellites including Sentinel-1, SAOCOM, and ALOS-2, 
alongside ground-based radar data, to improve flood detection accuracy in 
complex environments including vegetated floodplains, open grasslands, and 
wetlands.

**Research Highlights:**
- Novel polarimetric change detection methods for flood extent mapping, 
optimised across reference image selection strategies and polarimetric 
decomposition approaches
- Multi-sensor flood mapping validation using optical, drone, and satellite 
data
- Controlled ground-based radar experiment synthesising SAR data to test 
flood scenarios under different surface and vegetation conditions
- Interactive web application automating Sentinel-1-based flood extent 
mapping for non-specialist users, tested across the UK, Guyana, Kenya, 
and Malawi

**Outputs:** 3 first-author peer-reviewed articles in IEEE Journal of 
Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing 
(IEEE JSTARS); presentations at IGARSS, EUSAR, EGU, ESA PolInSAR & 
Biomass, InGARSS, and AGU.

### Water Hyacinth Monitoring in Lake Victoria
**Institution:** University of Stirling  
**Duration:** 2022 – 2024

Applied polarimetric SAR methods to monitor invasive water hyacinth in 
Lake Victoria — an environmental challenge with significant consequences 
for communities and ecosystems across the region. This work demonstrates 
how radar remote sensing can support ecosystem management where optical 
data is limited by cloud cover.

**Key Achievements:**
- Developed dual-pol SAR methodology for water hyacinth detection and 
temporal monitoring
- Temporal analysis revealing seasonal infestation patterns across 
the lake
- Published in IEEE JSTARS (2024)
- Collaboration with the International Crops Research Institute for 
the Semi-Arid Tropics (ICRISAT)
- Presented at IGARSS 2024, EUSAR 2024, and InGARSS 2023

## Operational Systems

### Tanzania Streamflow Monitoring and Forecasting System
**Organisation:** SERVIR Eastern & Southern Africa, RCMRD  
**Duration:** 2019 – 2022

Led the development of an operational streamflow monitoring and forecasting 
system for Tanzanian river basins, with the Great Ruaha basin as the primary 
implementation site. The system supports water resources management, 
hydropower planning, and flood early warning.

**System Features:**
- Variable Infiltration Capacity (VIC) hydrological model with full 
land-surface parameterisation and multi-gauge calibration
- Integration of satellite precipitation datasets (GPM/IMERG, TAMSAT, 
CHIRPS)
- Streamflow forecasting with performance evaluation against 
USGS-equivalent gauge observations (NSE, RMSE, bias)
- Integration with GEOGLOWS global streamflow forecasting service

**Impact:** Intended for use by Tanzania's water management authorities 
for water allocation, hydropower planning, and flood early warning support.

### Rapid Flood Damage Assessment — Western Kenya (2020)
**Organisation:** SERVIR Eastern & Southern Africa, RCMRD  
**Duration:** 2020

Led rapid flood damage assessment following the April–May 2020 flooding 
in Western Kenya using Google Earth Engine and multisource satellite 
imagery. The analysis quantified impacts on agriculture, infrastructure, 
and settlements to support emergency response and recovery planning.

**Methodology:**
- Sentinel-1 SAR for flood extent mapping
- Sentinel-2 optical imagery for damage classification
- Google Earth Engine cloud-based processing
- Integration with population and infrastructure datasets

**Impact:** Delivered satellite-derived flood extent products to government 
and humanitarian agencies under operational conditions.

### Community-Based Flood Early Warning Systems — Malawi
**Organisation:** SERVIR Eastern & Southern Africa / UNDP Malawi  
**Duration:** 2020 – 2021

Led the implementation of Community-Based Flood Early Warning Systems 
(CBFEWS) in Malawi to enhance local flood preparedness and response. 
Conducted flood risk assessments, site scoping, system design, and 
supervised installation of telemetric water-level monitoring devices. 
Integrated ground-based monitoring with Sentinel-1 SAR-derived flood 
extents and satellite streamflow forecasts (GEOGLOWS) to improve 
forecasting accuracy and increase early warning lead time for 
vulnerable communities.

**Key Contributions:**
- Conducted flood hazard and risk assessments to identify priority 
intervention sites
- Designed and deployed telemetric river gauge systems for real-time 
water level monitoring
- Integrated Sentinel-1 SAR-derived flood extents with satellite 
streamflow forecasts into community-level warning frameworks
- Developed Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for community 
flood response
- Strengthened institutional capacity for flood preparedness and 
response across district and national levels

### Water Quality and Quantity Mapping — Kenya
**Client:** SNV Netherlands Development Organisation  
**Duration:** 2021  
**Location:** Laikipia, Isiolo, and Samburu Counties, Kenya

Conducted an integrated regional water resource assessment across three 
arid and semi-arid counties of Kenya. The project combined field 
sampling, remote sensing analysis, and GIS mapping to assess water 
availability and quality for pastoral communities.

**Key Deliverables:**
- Baseline water quality assessment across physical, chemical, and 
bacteriological parameters
- Mapping of water points and seasonal water availability
- Analysis of water demand patterns across pastoral communities
- Recommendations for sustainable water resources management
