## Journal Articles

**Isundwa, F. K.**, Marino, A., Berardi, A., Bovolo, I., Hunter, P., 
Neil, C., Perez, C. S., & Silva, T. S. F. (2026). *Analytical 
Optimisation of Polarimetric Change Detectors for Mapping Open and 
Grassland Flooding Using Sentinel-1: Case of Rupununi, Guyana*. 
**IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and 
Remote Sensing**. **Accepted for publication.**  
Analytically optimised polarimetric change detectors for mapping 
flooding in open and grassland environments, applied to the North 
Rupununi wetlands in Guyana using Sentinel-1 data.

---

**Isundwa, F. K.**, Marino, A., Berardi, A., Bovolo, I., Hunter, P., 
Neil, C., Perez, C. S., & Silva, T. S. F. (2025). *Optimizing Change 
Detection Methods for Flood Mapping Using Polarimetric SAR*. 
**IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and 
Remote Sensing**.  
[https://doi.org/10.1109/JSTARS.2025.3628450](https://doi.org/10.1109/JSTARS.2025.3628450)  
Systematically optimised polarimetric SAR change detection approaches 
for flood extent mapping, benchmarking reference image selection 
strategies and polarimetric methods across the 2023 Emilia-Romagna 
flood in Italy and a validation case in Scotland.

---

**Isundwa, F. K.**, Marino, A., Simpson, M. D., Akbari, V., 
Silva, T. S. F., Datta, A., Nagendra, P. G., Pranuthi, G., & 
Srikanth, R. (2024). *Mapping and Monitoring of Water Hyacinth in 
Lake Victoria Using Polarimetric Radar Data*. **IEEE Journal of 
Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing**.  
[https://doi.org/10.1109/jstars.2024.3476938](https://doi.org/10.1109/jstars.2024.3476938)  
Applied polarimetric SAR decomposition for invasive aquatic vegetation 
monitoring in Lake Victoria, East Africa, demonstrating seasonal 
infestation dynamics across the lake basin. Collaboration with 
ICRISAT.

---

Macharia, D., Mugabo, L., **Kasiti, F. I.**, Noriega, A., 
MacDonald, L., & Thomas, E. (2023). *Streamflow and Flood Prediction 
in Rwanda Using Machine Learning and Remote Sensing*. 
**Frontiers in Climate**.  
[https://doi.org/10.3389/fclim.2023.1158186](https://doi.org/10.3389/fclim.2023.1158186)  
Applied machine learning and satellite remote sensing for operational 
streamflow and flood forecasting to support rural infrastructure 
resilience in Rwanda.

---

**Isundwa, F. K.**, et al. (2019). *The Potential for Water Stewardship 
Partnership in Kenya*. **Arabian Journal of Geosciences**.  
[https://doi.org/10.1007/s12517-019-4506-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12517-019-4506-x)  
Assessed opportunities for multi-stakeholder water stewardship 
frameworks in Kenya, combining hydrological data and policy analysis.

## Conference Proceedings

**Isundwa, F. K.**, Marino, A., Silva, T. S. F., Bovolo, I., 
Berardi, A., & Neil, C. (2024). *Mapping Floods Using SAR Polarimetry 
in Imola, Italy*. In **IGARSS 2024 — IEEE International Geoscience 
and Remote Sensing Symposium**, pp. 10799–10802.  
[https://doi.org/10.1109/igarss53475.2024.10640708](https://doi.org/10.1109/igarss53475.2024.10640708)  
Demonstrated polarimetric SAR-based flood extent mapping in an urban 
floodplain environment using the 2023 Imola, Italy flood event, 
evaluating four change detection methods against PlanetScope 
optical reference data.

---

**Isundwa, F. K.**, Simpson, M., Akbari, V., Datta, A., Nagendra, P., 
Pranuthi, G., Silva, T., Srikanth, R., & Marino, A. (2024). 
*Monitoring Temporal Trends to Assess Infestation of Water Hyacinth 
in Lake Victoria Using PolSAR*. In **EUSAR 2024 — 15th European 
Conference on Synthetic Aperture Radar**, Munich, 23–26 April 2024.  
[https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10659438](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10659438)  
Time-series polarimetric analysis tracking seasonal water hyacinth 
dynamics across Lake Victoria.

---

**Isundwa, F. K.**, Simpson, M., Akbari, V., Datta, A., Nagendra, P., 
Pranuthi, G., Silva, T., Rupavatharam, S., & Marino, A. (2023). 
*Using PolSAR to Assess Temporal Trends of Water Hyacinth Infestation 
in Lake Victoria*. In **2023 IEEE India Geoscience and Remote Sensing 
Symposium (InGARSS)**, Bangalore, India, 10–13 December 2023.  
[https://doi.org/10.1109/ingarss59135.2023.10490369](https://doi.org/10.1109/ingarss59135.2023.10490369)  
Initial findings from multi-temporal polarimetric classification of 
aquatic vegetation infestation patterns in Lake Victoria.
