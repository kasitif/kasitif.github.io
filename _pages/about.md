I have spent over eight years working at the frontier of hydrology and water 
resources — and for the past three, pushing that frontier into polarimetric 
SAR remote sensing to turn radar signals into flood intelligence that reaches 
the people who need it.

My work sits at the intersection of polarimetric SAR remote sensing and 
operational hydrology. On the remote sensing side, I develop and publish novel 
change detection methods for flood mapping and aquatic vegetation monitoring 
using multiband SAR data — Sentinel-1, SAOCOM, and ALOS-2 — with three 
first-author papers in IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth 
Observations and Remote Sensing (IEEE JSTARS). On the hydrology side, I have 
led the operational deployment of process-based hydrological models (VIC, SWAT, 
EF5) for streamflow forecasting and water balance analysis across East African 
river basins under the NASA SERVIR programme.

That combination has meant building end-to-end community-based flood early 
warning systems in Malawi — integrating SAR-derived flood extents, telemetric 
sensors, and streamflow forecasts into systems that communities and governments 
actually use — and supporting operational flood damage assessments in Kenya 
under real emergency conditions.

I hold a competitively awarded ESA/CONAE PUMAS programme satellite tasking 
access to SAOCOM L-band SAR through 2027, and I am independently developing 
an open web application that puts Sentinel-1-based flood extent mapping into 
the hands of non-specialist users — tested across the UK, Guyana, Kenya, and 
Malawi.

I am committed to open, reproducible science and regularly share code and 
methods through GitHub.

## Research Interests

- **Polarimetric SAR** — change detection and signal decomposition for flood 
mapping in vegetated floodplains, wetlands, and open water surfaces
- **Multiband SAR** — exploiting C-band, L-band, and X-band differences for 
surface characterisation under flooding conditions
- **Operational Hydrology** — process-based streamflow forecasting integrating 
satellite precipitation, soil moisture, and land surface models
- **Flood Early Warning** — translating Earth observation data into actionable 
products for communities and operational flood managers
- **Machine Learning** — deep learning for automated flood mapping, damage 
assessment, and near-real-time situational awareness

## Career Highlights

**University of Stirling, NERC-funded PhD** *(2022–present)*  
Developing and publishing novel polarimetric SAR change detection methods for 
flood mapping across the UK, Italy, Guyana, and East Africa. Three first-author 
IEEE JSTARS papers. ESA/CONAE PUMAS awardee.

**Scottish Environment Protection Agency** *(Feb–Jul 2025, Placement)*  
Designed and implemented a near-real-time disaster intelligence system 
integrating satellite emergency mapping with deep learning-based media 
classification to support operational flood response across the UK.

**SERVIR Eastern & Southern Africa, RCMRD** *(2019–2022)*  
Led operational deployment of VIC-based streamflow forecasting system for 
Tanzania and implemented community-based flood early warning systems in Malawi 
under UNDP funding, combining Sentinel-1 SAR, telemetric sensors, and 
satellite streamflow forecasts.

**Hydrosan Limited, Kenya** *(2013–2018)*  
Led hydrological and hydrogeological assessments, catchment management 
planning, and environmental impact assessments across western Kenya.
